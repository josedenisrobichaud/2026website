[Élodie Clavet](authors/e-clavet/), Audrey Goyette and Mariane Gosselin, master’s students in veterinary science at the Rimouski campus, have won the New Transfer Initiative Grant offered by the organization [Op+lait](www.oplait.org).
This $5,000 grant will enable them to create the podcast “Dans l’fenil” to share agricultural research conducted in the region with the general public.

Congratulations, ladies! We can’t wait to listen to you.
