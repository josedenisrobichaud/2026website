We were pleased to participate in the 19th Symposium of the Quebec Reproduction Network (RQR), held in Montreal from October 19 to 21, 2026. Each year, this event brings together researchers, students, and partners in the field of reproduction to share their work and discuss current issues in the field.

This year, the symposium provided a great opportunity to showcase projects led by our team. José had the chance to contribute to the pre-symposium session focused on collaborations between industry and research, a topic particularly close to her heart, as it helps bridge the gap between scientific discoveries and real-world applications. She co-presented with Débora Santschi (Valacta), and our presentation allowed us to discuss the importance of involving industry stakeholders early in the development of a research project.

The conference also featured a poster presentation by one of our summer interns, Zachary Sinclair, who was able to share the results of his project with researchers and students from various institutions. Beyond simply disseminating results, these gatherings often represent important milestones in the careers of young researchers. They provide an opportunity to receive constructive feedback, discover new approaches, and build a professional network that can support them throughout their careers.

Participating in events like the RQR Symposium reminds us of the importance of collaboration and scientific exchange. We return inspired by the discussions, new ideas, and connections forged with members of this dynamic research community.
